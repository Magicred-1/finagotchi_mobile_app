# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Finagotchi is a mobile app for people who want a light, daily habit layered on top of Solana. They open the app once a day to check in, watch their creature grow, and keep a streak alive. The core context is short sessions on a phone — often one-handed, sometimes distracted, usually with a smile.

## Product Purpose

Turn wallet connection and daily check-ins into a creature-raising experience. The app makes blockchain interaction feel personal and low-stakes by wrapping it in a Tamagotchi-style pet that evolves with consistency.

## Brand Personality

Retro, crunchy, energetic.

The interface should feel like a modern handheld toy: punchy feedback, crisp transitions, and just enough charm to make the daily loop feel rewarding. Not soft or squishy; the motion has bite and texture.

## Anti-references

- Over-bouncy elastic toys: avoid excessive wobble, springy overshoot, or physics that feel cheap and childish.
- Corporate SaaS minimalism: the app should not feel sterile or emotionally flat.
- Crypto neon chaos: no flashing, aggressive, or overloaded motion.

## Design Principles

1. **Motion is feedback, not decoration.** Every animation should answer a user action or state change.
2. **One-handed clarity.** Interactive elements and feedback must feel immediate on a phone.
3. **Delight in the loop.** The daily check-in should have a small, satisfying moment without dragging the session out.
4. **Consistency over novelty.** Reuse motion language across the app so it feels like one cohesive device.
5. **Respect the user.** Reduced-motion preferences are honored; essential feedback can stay, but decorative loops stop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Honor system `prefers-reduced-motion` settings.
- Keep touch targets at least 44×44 pt.
- Maintain sufficient contrast for text and interactive elements on the dark background.
